SYS_ENG_02 xAPI // TELEMETRY LRS

Enterprise Learning Record Store & Analytics

Operational Friction:

Disjointed learner activity logs dispersed across multiple 3rd-party SaaS platforms created critical visibility blind spots, preventing executive talent analytics and regulatory compliance checks.

Architecture & Tech Stack:

High-throughput asynchronous xAPI event receiver, decoupled queue workers, partitioned PostgreSQL database with JSONB indexing, and real-time streaming to downstream data warehouses.

Quantified Business Outcome:

Delivered consolidated global reporting engine handling 10M+ events with sub-50ms ingestion latency and zero packet drop.
